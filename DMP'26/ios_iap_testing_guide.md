# Guide: End-to-End iOS In-App Purchase Testing

This guide outlines how to perform end-to-end testing of your subscriptions and lifetime purchases on a real iOS device using Apple Sandbox accounts.

---

## 1. App Store Connect Setup

To test native purchases, Apple requires the product identifiers to exist in the App Store Connect portal.

1. **Log in to App Store Connect** ([appstoreconnect.apple.com](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com)).
2. **Select your App** (matching your bundle ID, e.g. `com.aspiring.PocketBudget`).
3. Go to **Features** -> **In-App Purchases** / **Subscriptions**:
    - Ensure you have registered the following products with their corresponding App Store Connect localization configurations (fitted to Apple's character limits: **Display Name: max 35 chars, Description: max 55 chars**):
      *   **`com.pocketbudget.subscription.monthly` (Auto-Renewable Subscription)**:
          *   **Display Name**: `Monthly Premium Plan` (20 chars)
          *   **Description**: `Unlock unlimited budget sheets & exports monthly.` (49 chars)
      *   **`com.pocketbudget.subscription.annual` (Auto-Renewable Subscription)**:
          *   **Display Name**: `Annual Premium Plan` (19 chars)
          *   **Description**: `Unlock unlimited budget sheets & exports yearly.` (48 chars)
      *   **`com.pocketbudget.lifetime` (Non-Consumable Purchase)**:
          *   **Display Name**: `Lifetime Premium Plan` (21 chars)
          *   **Description**: `Lifetime access to unlimited sheets & PDF exports.` (50 chars)
    - **Subscription Group**: Auto-renewable subscriptions must belong to a subscription group. When setting up the group:
        *   **Subscription Group Display Name** (Max 35 chars): Use `Pocket Budget Premium` or `Premium Access`.
        *   **App Name Display Option**: Select **"Use App Name Pocket Budget Pro"**.
    - Ensure each product has a price tier configured (e.g., $0.99 for monthly, $9.99 for annual, $34.99 for lifetime) and the status is **"Ready to Submit"** or **"Approved"**.

## 2. Create a Sandbox Tester Account

Apple handles test purchases through dedicated Sandbox tester profiles.

1. In App Store Connect, go to **Users and Access**.
2. Under the **Sandbox** section in the left sidebar, select **Testers**.
3. Click the **"+" button** to add a new tester.
4. Fill in the details (requires a unique email address not registered as a standard Apple ID).
5. Set your App Store territory/region.

---

## 3. Configure Your Test iOS Device

1. Connect your real iPhone or iPad to your Mac via USB.
2. Open the device's **Settings** app.
3. Scroll down and tap **App Store**.
4. Scroll all the way to the bottom to find the **Sandbox Account** section.
5. Tap on it and sign in with the **Sandbox Tester email** you created in Step 2.
   
> [!NOTE]
> Do NOT sign out of your main Apple ID at the top of Settings. iOS maintains sandbox tester accounts separately under the App Store settings.

## 5. Debug Console Logs (Real-time Safari Inspector)

To inspect `console.log` logs from the device:

1. Open **Safari** on your Mac.
2. In the top menu, go to **Develop** -> **[Your Device Name]** -> **localhost (or index.html)**.
   - If the "Develop" menu is missing in Safari, turn it on in *Safari Settings -> Advanced -> Show features for web developers*.
3. Safari Web Inspector will pop up. You can view all logs, network validation requests, and inspect the DOM directly in real time.

## 6. End-to-End Purchase Verification Flow

Once the app is running on your phone, test the following flows:

### Test 1: Subscribe to Monthly Plan
1. Go to the Premium Page.
2. Tap **Subscribe** on the Monthly Plan.
3. The App Store sheet will slide up from the bottom showing `[Environment: Sandbox]`.
4. Double-click to pay or authenticate.
5. Watch the Safari console logs:
   - `[IAP] Transaction approved for com.pocketbudget.subscription.monthly, verifying...`
   - `[IAP] Resolved product ID for validation: com.pocketbudget.subscription.monthly`
   - `[IAP] Entitling product: com.pocketbudget.subscription.monthly`
6. The UI should instantly refresh, showing the Active Banner indicating your Monthly plan is active and showing the correct renewal date (e.g. 30 days from now).

### Test 2: Stack/Extend with Annual Plan (Upgrade Flow)
1. Since you have a monthly subscription active, the selection grid is still visible.
2. Tap **Subscribe** on the Annual Plan.
3. Authenticate the Sandbox purchase.
4. Look at the console logs and UI:
   - The service will stack the Annual duration (365 days) on top of your existing monthly expiration date.
   - The UI banner and cards will instantly update the renews date to reflect the extended expiry (+395 days total).

### Test 3: Manage Subscription
1. Click the **Manage** button on the Active Subscription Banner.
2. Verify that it launches Apple's native App Store subscriptions settings view directly on your phone.

## 7. Testing via TestFlight (Without Xcode or Cable)

If you want to test the build on a device without a Mac/Xcode connection (e.g., for external testers or team members), you can use Apple's **TestFlight**:

### Step 1: Upload the Build
1. In Xcode, select **Any iOS Device (arm64)** as the target.
2. Go to the top menu and select **Product** -> **Archive**.
3. Once the archive completes, click **Distribute App** in the Organizer window.
4. Upload it to **App Store Connect**.

### Step 2: Set up TestFlight in App Store Connect
1. Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com).
2. Select your app, and go to the **TestFlight** tab.
3. Under the **Internal Testing** sidebar section, click **App Store Connect Users** (or create an external testing group).
4. Add yourself/testers to the list. They will receive an email invitation.

### Step 3: Install and Test on iOS Device
1. Download the **TestFlight** app from the App Store on your iPhone/iPad.
2. Open TestFlight, sign in with your Apple ID, and accept the invitation email/link.
3. Tap **Install** next to the Pocket Budget build.

### ⚠️ TestFlight Purchase Behavior:
*   **Sandbox Environment**: All builds distributed through TestFlight run in Apple's **Sandbox mode** by default.
*   **Automatic Sandbox Mapping**: You do **not** need to sign into a separate Sandbox tester account under your phone settings. TestFlight purchases use your **real Apple ID**, but Apple routes the transaction to the sandbox server.
*   **No Charges**: When you tap to buy a subscription or plan, a prompt will appear stating: *"You're all set. Note: This purchase is made in the sandbox environment and will not charge your account."*
*   **Simulated Subscriptions Time**: Auto-renewable subscriptions expire much faster in TestFlight/Sandbox to facilitate testing:
    *   1 Month subscription -> expires in **5 minutes**
    *   1 Year subscription -> expires in **1 hour**

## 8. How to Test Cancellation, Expiration, and Resets

By default, sandbox subscriptions automatically renew up to 6 times per day before stopping. You can manually force a cancellation, expiration, or clear purchase history using the following methods:

### Method A: For Real Sandbox & TestFlight Testing
If testing on a real device linked to App Store Connect, you can manage or reset active subscriptions:

1.  **Cancel Auto-Renewal from iPhone Settings**:
    *   On your test device, go to **Settings** -> **App Store**.
    *   Scroll to the bottom and tap your **Sandbox Account** email address.
    *   Tap **Manage Subscriptions**.
    *   Select your active plan and tap **Cancel Subscription**. Once cancelled, the subscription will not renew at the end of the current sandbox interval (e.g. at the end of the next 5-minute block, it will expire, and your app's premium status should automatically be removed).
2.  **Clear/Reset Purchase History (Start Fresh)**:
    *   Log in to App Store Connect and go to **Users and Access** -> **Sandbox** -> **Testers** (in the left sidebar).
    *   Select the checkbox next to your test account.
    *   Click **Clear Purchase History** at the top. This instantly wipes all active purchases and subscriptions from that user's history, allowing you to test a clean "unsubscribed" state immediately.

### Method B: For Local Xcode StoreKit Testing (Fastest Method)
If you are running the app from Xcode with a `.storekit` configuration file active, Xcode provides a powerful **StoreKit Transaction Manager** that lets you control active states in real time:

1.  **Open the Transaction Manager**:
    *   While the app is running, go to the bottom debug bar in Xcode and click the **StoreKit Transaction Manager icon** (or go to the top menu and select **Debug** -> **StoreKit** -> **Manage Transactions...**).
2.  **Force Actions**:
    *   A list of all active local purchases will load.
    *   Select your active Monthly or Annual subscription.
    *   Use the buttons at the top of the Transaction Manager window to perform:
        *   **Delete/Refund**: Wipes the purchase instantly.
        *   **Cancel**: Disables auto-renewal so it expires at the end of the interval.
        *   **Force Expiry**: Instantly expires the subscription, which lets you see if the app immediately blocks premium features.
